SIMULATION PHILOSOPHY
Players are not reduced to one statistical overall. The simulation evaluates: physicality, work ethic, dominance, hardness, basketball IQ, adaptability, skill, defense, clutch and leadership.

The actual game simulation then converts those characteristics into possessions, shots, rebounds, assists, turnovers, fouls, defensive plays and matchup results.

90s environment: physical perimeter defense, hand-checking, slower half-court possessions, no modern defensive three-second rule and increased emphasis on post play, rebounding and physical defense.
