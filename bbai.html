The game begins with a draft. You and the AI take turns selecting all-time players. After the draft, both sides can trade, adjust lineups, identify weaknesses and enter a best-of-seven series.


SIMULATION PHILOSOPHY

The engine does not simply ask who has the highest career statistics. Players are modeled through physical ability, work ethic, dominance, hardness, basketball IQ, adaptability, skill, defensive intelligence, leadership and clutch mentality. The 90s ruleset then changes how those traits interact.
